About the Brand

Shrishti Solutions

Shrishti Solutions is a leading corporate wellness consultancy dedicated to promoting health, productivity, and work-life balance for modern organizations. Their comprehensive wellness programs combine personalized coaching, digital tracking, and community engagement to help employees thrive.

When Shrishti Solutions partnered with Aqua Nova, they envisioned a distinctive branding experience that would extend their wellness message beyond the office walls. What began as simple bottle customization evolved into a complete QR-enabled customer experience, connecting employees, clients, and partners through every touchpoint.

Wellness First

Aqua Nova bottles champion health-first messaging with Shrishti's signature blue branding and QR-linked wellness content.

Smart QR Connected

Each bottle features a QR code linking to a personalized member portal with wellness tips, progress tracking, and event details.

Wellness Programs & Resources
