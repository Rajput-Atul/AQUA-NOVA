# Aqua Nova

A lightweight, frontend-only showcase for customized clear PET water bottles and their QR-powered customer experiences.

## Run locally

Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve this folder with any static file server. Vercel rewrites `/c/:slug` to the app entry point so the sample customer routes work on direct visits and refreshes. The production site is available at [aqua-nova-theta.vercel.app](https://aqua-nova-theta.vercel.app).

## Included

- Responsive product and branding showcase for 250 ml, 500 ml and 1 litre bottles.
- Interactive quote brief that opens a WhatsApp enquiry.
- Sample customer pages at `/c/shrishti-solutions`, `/c/bluebird-cafe` and `/c/summit-2027`.
- Customer vCard download and sample menu category navigation.
- SEO metadata, robots.txt, sitemap.xml, favicon and Vercel route rewrites.

All data is static and local to the frontend. The sample pages demonstrate the customer experience; live editable QR destinations, customer administration, order processing and pricing require a future backend. Connect the actual business WhatsApp number and configure a production domain before distributing physical QR labels.
